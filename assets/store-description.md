# Home Carousel Zoom

Enlarge the selected game cover in the Steam Deck Home carousel. Zoom works with controller focus and pointer hover. The focus ring and attached title move with the cover; the Library grid and the **View more in your Library** card keep their normal size.

## Controls

- **Zoom:** 100%–210%, with separate base and fine sliders. Default: 115%. Fine adjustment uses 1% steps.
- **Focus appearance:** keep Steam's default effect, or choose a custom highlight color with adjustable alpha.
- **Custom outline:** 1–10 px thickness.
- **Custom halo:** 0–30 px blur, in 5 px steps. Set it to 0 to keep the outline without an added halo.
- **Artwork glow:** keep Steam's artwork-colored effect, or override its opacity from 0% to 100% in 10% steps. This control works in both focus modes and is separate from the custom halo.

Custom styling is optional. Settings are saved, and disabling the theme removes its styling. The zoom animation respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Notes

Large zoom values can overlap nearby cards and text. On the stock Steam layout, high zoom can extend above the screen; lower the zoom if the cover does not fit.

When updating from v1.0.0, set the new zoom sliders again. The old single-slider value does not transfer automatically.

## Preview

The preview shows 115% zoom, a cyan custom highlight at 80% alpha, a 2 px outline, a 15 px halo, and artwork opacity at 0%. Home Carousel Zoom was the only enabled CSS Loader theme. Cover badges come from other installed plugins and are not included.
